Research question: Is the reception of 2026 Worlds teasers measurably more negative than 2023-2025, using only comments posted within a fixed window after each teaser’s release?
Primary metric: the share of comments that criticize the teaser itself.
Secondary metrics: share that praise it, share that compare it to earlier teasers, and the like-to-view ratio.
Comment window: the first 72 hours
What would count as no difference: If the [emotion] score of two teasers are within [x] of another, then the reception score is counted as no difference
